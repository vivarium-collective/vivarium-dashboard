## Why this is needed

OOM/memory-growth risk scales with GENERATION DEPTH, 
not seed count — a 2-4 seed x full-10-generation pilot exercises the exact same danger zone 
as a 30-seed one, at a fraction of the cost/time, since each seed independently runs all 10 
generations regardless of concurrency. Contention/bin-packing risk (the other axis) is 
exactly what item 33's fix should have addressed, so this pilot doesn't need to re-prove 
30-way concurrency safety from scratch — it needs to confirm the fix actually works at small 
scale first.

**This pilot is also the first opportunity, ever, to check whether the analysis auto-trigger
fires correctly for whichever dependency shape item 33 lands on** — genuinely unverified as
of this writing; no dispatch using the Array-jobs shape has ever reached a clean terminal
SUCCEEDED (sim139 died at 20/30 disk failures, sim140 was killed after the redo-from-scratch
discovery). If item 33 picks individual jobs, this is also the first real test of the new
polling-fan-in analysis-dependency mechanism described in item 33.
