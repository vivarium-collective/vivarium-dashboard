## Goal

Either (a) the workbench pod needs a REAL sms-ecoli/v2ecoli
install kept in sync with what it dispatches against (not a disconnected stub), or (b) at
minimum, `remote_run_submit`/`remote_run_land` must surface `analysis_errors` back to the

this item is really about making that dispatch-time validation loud, not silent, regardless
of which underlying fix (a) or (b) is chosen.

**REAL ROOT CAUSE FOUND ONE LAYER DEEPER, supersedes PR #786/#787's approach**: re-read

`ws_root` in scope.

`build_analysis_options()`, call `_ws_add_to_sys_path(ws_root)` before the import. Once
