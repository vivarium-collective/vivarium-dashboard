that the mechanism used for fully-remote canonical dispatch is comprehensively and holistically
generalized to semantically link, merge, and/or unionize viva-api's concept of a "Simulator" with
vivarium-workbench's concept of a "Module" — and, peripherally, with lower priority, with
viva-marketplace's concept of the same, later on.

## Why this is needed

The real MVP deliverable of the ecosystem, as previously described, involves native-process-bigraph-
and-viva-api design as a non-negotiable, fundamental tenet of its design.
